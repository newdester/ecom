      Built For Car Lovers

      Not Just Another Model On A Shelf

      Motor Scent is a working humidifier with the body of a sports car, bike or truck. Fill the tank, tap the button, and the headlights come on while cool mist rolls out of the exhaust.

      18 Models

      From a Red Bull F1 car to a GT3 RS, an RS6, a Ram TRX, a G-Wagon or a desert tank. There is one for every garage.

      Quiet Enough For The Bedroom

      Whisper-quiet ultrasonic mist, USB powered, auto shut-off when the tank is empty.

      Real Mist, No Heat

      Fill. Tap. Watch it idle.

      Pour water into the hidden tank, press the button once and the ultrasonic plate turns it into cool mist that rolls out of the exhaust. No heat, no smell, safe on any desk.

      Runs for hours. One fill lasts an evening, and it shuts off by itself when the tank is empty.

      Add a scent. A few drops of lavender oil in the tank and the whole room smells like it.

      Frequently Asked Questions

      check_box

      How does it work?

      Fill the tank with water, plug in the USB cable and press the button. The ultrasonic plate turns the water into cool mist that comes out of the exhaust while the headlights light up.

      check_box

      Is it real smoke?

      No. It is cool water mist, the same as a regular humidifier. No heat, no smell, safe on any desk or shelf.

      check_box

      Can I add scent?

      Yes. Add a few drops of the Lavender Oil (or any water-soluble essential oil) to the tank and the mist carries the scent.

      check_box

      How big is it?

      About the size of a 1:24 scale model car, roughly 20 cm long. It fits on a desk, nightstand or shelf.

      check_box

      How long does shipping take?

      Orders ship within 1-3 business days and usually arrive in 7-12 business days. You get a tracking link by email.

      check_box

      What's the return policy?

      30 days. If you are not happy with your Motor Scent, contact us and we sort it out.
